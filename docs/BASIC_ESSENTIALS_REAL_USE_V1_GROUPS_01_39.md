## Product rule

The Basic Essentials post-group practice model should become:

LearningGroup → exactly ONE Real Use Unit

Real Use Unit types:

- PHRASE: high-frequency combinations or short chunks.
- SENTENCE: high-frequency short sentences or replies.
- MICRO_SCENE: 2–4 connected lines only when the concepts naturally solve a real moment together.

Coverage is not the goal. Reality is the goal. One natural high-value sentence is better than three forced sentences.
